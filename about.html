The room

Built next to the book.

Two of us. One house. Birds and herps. Records in Broodstock. We are early on purpose — the room grows only as fast as the animals stay well.

Who runs it

KD Exotics is the breeding room. Broodstock is the software built beside it so clutch dates do not live in a text thread. One of us runs the room. One of us runs the book. Same house.

We are in the Treasure Valley. First time we will stand the room in public is the Boise reptile show, October 17–18, 2026.

What we will not skip

Small batch

After it leaves

When we do place an animal, the care sheet and the record go with it. We stay reachable.
