About Us

A husband-and-husband team, hands-on with every clutch

KD Exotics LLC is a small-batch bird and reptile breeding operation. No warehouse of racks, no volume quotas — just two people who like doing this well.

Our story

Where the name comes from

KD Exotics is named for the two of us — David and Keaton — and it started the way a lot of breeding operations do: with animals we already loved, and a lot of research on how to raise the next generation better than the last.

What began with a single reptile setup grew, clutch by clutch, into a operation covering both reptiles and birds. We keep it deliberately small: fewer pairs, more attention on each one, and enough time to actually handle and socialize every baby before it goes to a new home.
